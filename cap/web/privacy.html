Privacy Policy for Sky Skirmish

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What this game stores

Sky Skirmish stores your game progress (levels, XP, diamonds, settings, pilot name) only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, upload, or sell your personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID of your device and coarse device information to serve and measure ads and to detect fraud. This data is collected by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings: Android > Settings > Privacy > Ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Diamond packs and the optional "Remove ads" purchase are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; we never see or store your card or payment information. Google shares the fact that a purchase was completed so the game can grant your diamonds. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Children

Sky Skirmish is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data deletion

Your game progress can be erased at any time from Settings > Reset progress. Advertising data held by Google can be managed in your device ad settings or your Google account.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the developer through the Google Play store listing for Sky Skirmish.
